## Scope note

The patterns come from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing", which is written about English. The
structural ones - dashes as the universal connector, forced triads, not-X-but-Y, one-line closers,
staged run-up, inflated significance - are rhetorical and apply to Polish as well. The vocabulary
ones, such as stock AI words and avoiding is/are/has, are English-specific and will not fire on
Polish text.
